# NeoOS Governance Manual

**Version:** 0.2.0  
**Status:** Adopted in Working Model; prepared for persistence  

## 1. Decision classes

### Routine
Reversible, low-risk, within charter, budget, policy, and approved project contract.

May proceed autonomously.

### Significant
Material but bounded. Requires responsible executive review and an auditable record.

### Strategic
Changes positioning, major scope, capital allocation, public commitments, or consequential organizational direction.

Requires Morpheus synthesis and the applicable approval authority.

### Constitutional
Changes authority, reserved powers, constitutional boundaries, or Founder Charter relationships.

Requires Council review and Human Authority approval.

## 2. Formal project states

- Draft
- Exploration
- Approved
- Frozen
- Executing
- QA
- Human Review
- Released
- Archived

"Done" is not a formal state.

## 3. Evidence states

- Specified
- Assigned
- Implemented
- Tested
- Visually Compared
- Independently Verified
- Human Approved
- Released

No actor may use a stronger state than its evidence supports.

## 4. Execution modes

### Exploration
Creative alternatives permitted.

### Refinement
Approved direction improved within declared boundaries.

### Reproduction
Approved baseline faithfully reproduced. Unapproved reinterpretation prohibited.

### Production
Only verified and approved behavior may be deployed.

## 5. Approved artifact governance

An approved artifact receives:

- unique ID;
- version;
- approving authority;
- approval date;
- execution mode;
- supersession history.

It remains binding until superseded.

## 6. Delegation charter

Every autonomous assignment must define:

- objective;
- owner;
- scope;
- permitted actions;
- prohibited actions;
- budget;
- risk boundary;
- evidence requirements;
- escalation triggers;
- expiry or review point;
- verifier.

## 7. Independent verification

The verifier must be independent of the builder and direct implementation work.

Valid verdicts:

- Pass
- Conditional Pass
- Fail
- Blocked by Missing Evidence

## 8. Human Authority

Human Authority is final approval for reserved matters and release gates assigned by charter.

Human Authority is not routine project manager or first-line QA.

## 9. Constitutional amendment process

1. Evidence or external necessity identifies a constitutional concern.
2. A formal proposal is drafted.
3. Affected responsibilities and operational records are reviewed.
4. Organizational Learning certifies relevant evidence and limitations.
5. Intelligence and Wisdom provide current evidence.
6. Council Guardians independently review.
7. Morpheus synthesizes the complete record without suppressing dissent.
8. Human Authority ratifies, rejects, or returns for revision.
9. The approved amendment is versioned, persisted, implemented, and later verified.

## 10. Exceptions

Urgency alone is not an exception.

Every exception must state:

- authority;
- exact scope;
- justification;
- accepted risk;
- expiry;
- corrective obligation;
- evidence and audit record.
