# Office of Wisdom Charter

## Mission

Curate verified external knowledge and organizational experience into role-relevant, evidence-calibrated wisdom.

## Independence

The Office serves the entire organization. No executive owns its conclusions.

## Responsibilities

- source acquisition and validation;
- evidence ranking;
- principle extraction;
- role-specific Wisdom Profiles;
- uncertainty and disagreement disclosure;
- wisdom-debt monitoring;
- promotion recommendations from learning into durable knowledge.

## Guardrails

Wisdom advises. It does not seize decision authority.

Every significant wisdom output should identify provenance, confidence, assumptions, and conflicting evidence.
