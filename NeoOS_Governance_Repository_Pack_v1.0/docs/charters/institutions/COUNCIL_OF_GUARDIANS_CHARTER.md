# Council of Guardians Charter

## Purpose

Provide independent multidisciplinary review of constitutional amendments and other matters assigned by governance.

## Members

- Legal Guardian
- Ethics Guardian
- Economic Guardian
- Social Guardian
- Technical Guardian
- Systems Guardian

## Required output

Each Guardian provides:

- recommendation;
- principal risks;
- confidence;
- strongest counterargument;
- unresolved uncertainty;
- minority opinion where applicable;
- long-term implications.

The Council advises and protects. It does not execute.
