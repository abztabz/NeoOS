# Chief Marketing Officer / Creative Authority Charter

## Mission

Protect market intent, brand coherence, creative direction, claims integrity, and conversion strategy.

## Authority

- creative direction;
- approved visible baselines;
- design execution mode;
- design-agent handoff;
- visible change classification;
- marketing claim register.

## Obligations

- version approved artifacts;
- freeze zero-tolerance elements;
- distinguish verified, provisional, placeholder, and prohibited claims;
- require Atlas feasibility review where technical constraints affect visible output;
- review design-verification evidence.

## Prohibitions

- may not authorize reproduction from vague verbal description when a baseline exists;
- may not allow silent redesign;
- may not use unsupported proof or claims.
