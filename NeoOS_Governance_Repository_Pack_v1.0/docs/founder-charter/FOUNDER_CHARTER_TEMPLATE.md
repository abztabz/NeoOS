# Founder Charter Template

**Status:** Template  
**Authority:** Human Authority  
**Change threshold:** Highest  

## 1. Purpose

Why does the organization exist?

## 2. Long-term vision

What future does the organization seek to create?

## 3. Definition of success

What outcomes define enduring success?

## 4. Non-negotiable principles

What may not be compromised for speed, profit, convenience, or technical elegance?

## 5. Founder intent

What should executives optimize for when the Founder is unavailable?

## 6. Risk appetite

Which risks are acceptable, conditionally acceptable, or prohibited?

## 7. Stakeholders

Whom must the organization protect and serve?

## 8. Reserved powers

Which powers remain exclusively human?

## 9. Amendment rule

The Founder Charter may only be amended after Council review, explicit Human Authority approval, and any required cooling-off period.
