# Glossary
**Cash Opportunity Score:** attractiveness of waiting.  
**Capital Deployment Gauge:** recommended portion of investable cash to deploy.  
**Confidence:** trust in the analysis.  
**Intrinsic Value:** conservative estimate of economic value.  
**Margin of Safety:** discount to conservative intrinsic value.  
**NeoOS Opportunity Index:** broad market attractiveness.  
**Opportunity Capacity:** capital that can responsibly be deployed.  
**Strong Buy:** rare classification requiring complete evidence and substantial margin of safety.
