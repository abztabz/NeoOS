# Scoring Methodology
Assign each factor 0–100, apply weights, sum, then apply evidence caps and governance overrides. Final permitted score is the lower of weighted score and applicable cap.

Score measures attractiveness. Confidence measures trust in the assessment. A high score with low confidence must not trigger aggressive deployment.

Use conservative intrinsic-value ranges and relevant stress tests such as revenue decline, margin compression, higher rates, currency shocks, refinancing stress, and multiple compression.
