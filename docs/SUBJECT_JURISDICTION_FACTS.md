## 1 and 2 together: the two-pool finding

Not a contradiction. It is the ordinary shape of a frontier-market capital
account, and it is the most consequential structural fact in this engagement.

Value **already outside** Nepal may be held and deployed globally.
Value **inside** Nepal largely cannot leave.

So the capital is not one pool. It is two, and they do not freely combine:

| | Mobile pool | Immobile pool |
|---|---|---|
| Contains | UAE earnings, assets held outside Nepal | Nepal property, NPR balances, any Nepal business |
| Can fund | Global allocation | Only Nepal |
| Serves | Generational wealth creation | Family, home, Nepal obligations |
| Governed by | Global opportunity | Nepal's economy and capital account |

### Four consequences

**Deployable capital was overstated, and now is not.** `investableCash` counted
every liquid asset as fungible. Capital behind a control can be sold instantly
and the proceeds still fund nothing abroad — mobility is not liquidity.
Liquidity is about time; mobility is about whether the door exists. Fixed in
schema v6.2, with the immobile pool reported separately rather than deducted
silently.

**Remitting to Nepal is close to irreversible.** If money struggles to leave,
every transfer home is a permanent allocation to the immobile pool, not a
transfer between accounts. That belongs in front of the subject **before** the
decision, which is a product requirement, not an observation.

**NPR concentration is a convertibility exposure, not only a currency one.** A
currency that might fall and a currency you cannot exit are different risks, and
the second is worse. Combined with the INR peg, the immobile pool's purchasing
power is governed by monetary policy set in Delhi, and cannot be diversified away
from inside Nepal.

**The generational objective splits in two.** Global generational wealth is built
from the mobile pool. The immobile pool serves family, property and obligations
at home. Different horizons, different risk capacities, different questions —
and answering them as one produces advice that fits neither.

## 3: the second peg

The subject states NPR is pegged to INR. With AED pegged to USD, **neither of the
two currencies in this household's life floats**, and each imports monetary
conditions set by a larger neighbour that has no interest in this family.

This is why K1.3 (Rey) and K2.2 (Eichengreen) were added to the corpus. The
question is live twice over, not academic.

**To verify:** the rate, the mechanism, when it was set, and how it has held.

## 4: intent is not an instrument

"Property goes to my wife and son" is an **intention**. Whether it is also the
outcome depends on a valid written instrument under the law that would administer
the estate, and Nepal has its own succession law.

Two gaps follow, and neither is a legal question NeoOS may answer:

- **Is there a written will, valid where the property sits?** Intent without an
  instrument may or may not produce the intended result.
- **A wife and a son exist and are not in the household data.** Dependents change
  the reserve, the horizon, tolerable drawdown, and what preservation means. The
  system has been calculating without knowing who the capital is for.

## What NeoOS must do with this

1. Plan around the two pools, and never present a combined deployable figure.
2. Say "as stated by you, not verified" wherever these change an answer.
3. Warn before an outbound transfer to a restricted jurisdiction.
4. Never quote any of it as law.

## What to verify, in priority order

1. **The actual outward limit from Nepal** — an annual allowance, an approval
   route, or a bar. The difference decides whether the immobile pool can ever be

2. **Whether a valid will exists** for the Nepal property, under Nepali law.
3. **The NPR peg mechanics**, from Nepal Rastra Bank.
4. **Tax treatment of foreign income** for the subject's residence and
   citizenship combination.
