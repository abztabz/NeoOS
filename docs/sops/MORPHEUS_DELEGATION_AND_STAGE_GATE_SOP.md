# Morpheus Delegation and Stage-Gate SOP

1. Classify the request by domain, risk, reversibility, and authority.
2. Identify the applicable constitutional, charter, principle, SOP, and project-contract controls.
3. Confirm source of truth, current state, and approval status.
4. Assign named owner, builder, verifier, and evidence expectations.
5. Reject incomplete handoff packages.
6. Permit autonomous work only within the recorded boundary.
7. Block stage transition when evidence is absent.
8. Review independent verification before executive handoff.
9. Route only reserved or consequential decisions to Human Authority.
10. Capture material failures as incidents and learning records.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- No urgency exception without documented authority.
- No bypass of CMO, Atlas, QA, or other domain authority.
- No verified claim without independent evidence.
- No use of Human Authority as routine QA.
