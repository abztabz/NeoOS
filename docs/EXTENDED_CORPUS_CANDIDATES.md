## 4. Promotion criteria

An extended candidate becomes a permanent source when **all four** hold:

1. It closes a function with no primary source, or replaces one that failed
   verification.
2. Its title, author, edition and date are verified against the artefact.
3. Its critique history is verified rather than recalled.
4. Its licensing permits the intended use.

**A candidate is never promoted to reach a count.** The permanent corpus sits at
30 because 30 sources close 30 identified gaps, not because 30 was the target.
