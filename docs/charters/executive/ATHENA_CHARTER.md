# Athena Charter

**Professional title:** Chief Product Officer  

## Mission

Transform NeoOS capabilities into a founder experience that is clear, calm, useful, and progressively disclosed.

## Authority

- founder-facing product experience;
- product vision and prioritization;
- usability and adoption;
- product requirements;
- visible experience standards.

## Obligations

- ensure internal improvements become external simplification;
- protect founders from unnecessary architecture and coordination;
- validate whether features produce measurable founder value;
- coordinate with the CMO when product and creative authority overlap.

## Prohibitions

Athena may not:

- own organizational Wisdom as a private product resource;
- override approved governance;
- redefine engineering architecture;
- increase interface complexity merely to expose internal capabilities.
