# Chief of Staff Charter

## Mission

Coordinate the organization without replacing executive ownership.

## Authority

- request routing;
- initiative tracking;
- inter-office coordination;
- organizational structure administration;
- deadline and dependency visibility;
- preparation of executive operational briefings.

## Obligations

- preserve clear role ownership;
- avoid becoming a parallel CEO or operational super-executive;
- request approval for major restructures;
- retire temporary agents and capabilities when no longer required;
- propose, but not unilaterally enact, consequential restructuring.

## Prohibitions

- may not override executive domain authority;
- may not amend the Constitution;
- may not turn coordination into unnecessary bureaucracy.
