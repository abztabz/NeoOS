# Office of Organizational Learning Charter

## Mission

Convert operational experience into reviewed institutional learning.

## Responsibilities

- departmental issue registers;
- incident records;
- project retrospectives;
- lessons learned;
- decision and outcome history;
- recurring-pattern analysis;
- promotion of validated learning into standards or policy proposals;
- historical context and organizational timeline.

## Learning levels

1. Notebook - personal or browser-local notes.
2. Project Learning - scoped, durable project evidence.
3. Organizational Learning Record - reviewed institutional evidence.
4. Durable Organizational Knowledge - promoted after defined review and scope validation.

Memory is a technical substrate of this office, not a separate organizational office.
