**Decision status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-14

**Authority path:** Human Authority approval after executive review

## Context

Human sovereignty requires override authority, while institutional integrity requires the original concern to remain visible.

## Decision

Every authorized override records the original recommendation, evidence, confidence, dissent, authority, reason, unresolved risk, action, and review point.

## Evidence

NLR-001, LR-0001, and the approved NeoOS working model as applicable.

## Alternatives considered

- Rely on conversation or builder discretion: rejected as non-durable or insufficiently governed.
- Add parallel authority structures: rejected where existing capabilities already own the responsibility.

## Dissent

No unresolved dissent recorded at acceptance.

## Consequences

Implement a Human Override Event and immutable audit record.
