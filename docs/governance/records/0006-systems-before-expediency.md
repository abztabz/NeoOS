# GDR-0006 - Systems Before Expediency

**Decision status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-14  
**Version introduced:** Governance corpus 1.1.0  
**Authority path:** Human Authority approval after executive review  
**Persistence state:** Prepared; persisted after merge  
**Enforcement state:** Pending applicable controls  
**Verification state:** Pending  

## Context

Urgency and prototype labels were treated as implied reasons to weaken controls.

## Decision

Approved standards outrank short-term expediency, while repeated compliance delay creates an obligation to simplify or automate the control.

## Evidence

NLR-001, LR-0001, and the approved NeoOS working model as applicable.

## Alternatives considered

- Rely on conversation or builder discretion: rejected as non-durable or insufficiently governed.
- Add parallel authority structures: rejected where existing capabilities already own the responsibility.

## Dissent

No unresolved dissent recorded at acceptance.

## Consequences

Exceptions require authority, scope, risk, expiry, and corrective action.

## Migration notes

Update the relevant Constitution, principles, charters, SOPs, project contracts, validators, and implementation backlog.
